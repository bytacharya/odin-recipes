Garlic Butter Pasta


Garlic Butter Pasta, AI generated

Need something quick, filling, and ridiculously easy to make? This garlic butter pasta takes barely any effort and works great when you don't feel like cooking anything complicated.

Ingredients for 2

  • 200 g pasta
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • Half teaspoon black pepper
  • Half teaspoon chilli flakes
  • Half teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan
  • Half cup pasta water
  • Small handful of chopped coriander
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water according to the package instructions.
  2. Before draining, reserve about ½ cup of the pasta water.
  3. Melt the butter in a pan over low heat.
  4. Add the garlic and cook for 1-2 minutes. Don't let it burn.
  5. Add the black pepper and chilli flakes.
  6. Add the cooked pasta and toss everything together.
  7. Slowly add the pasta water while stirring until a light sauce forms.
  8. Turn off the heat and add the Parmesan.
  9. Add the lemon juice and chopped coriander or parsley.
  10. Taste and adjust the salt and pepper.
  11. Serve hot.
