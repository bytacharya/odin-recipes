Cheese Omelette


Cheese Omlette, AI generated image

A quick, filling omelette made with eggs, cheese, and a few basic seasonings. It takes only a few minutes to prepare and is great for breakfast or a light meal.

Ingredients for 1

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons grated cheese
  • 1 tablespoon chopped onion
  • 1 tablespoon chopped coriander
  • Quarter teaspoon black pepper
  • Quarter teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon butter or oil

Instructions

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
  2. Add salt and black pepper, then beat the eggs well.
  3. Heat butter or oil in a pan over medium heat.
  4. Add the chopped onion and cook for about 1 minute.
  5. Pour the eggs into the pan.
  6. Cook until the bottom starts to set.
  7. Sprinkle the cheese and coriander over the eggs.
  8. Fold the omelette in half.
  9. Cook for another minute until the cheese melts.
  10. Serve hot.
